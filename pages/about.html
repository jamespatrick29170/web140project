About me

My name is James Beebe and I'm originally from Lebanon, NH a town about 10 minutes drive south of Dartmouth. I moved to Georgia in my early twenties and recently relocated to South Carolina. I created this site as part of my coursework in Web 140, at Campus, an online college. Long term I'd like to learn more about related disciplines like backend development and game development. I'd also like to pursue a Bachelor's in computer science or information technology once I complete this degree program.

I originally got into web development after deciding to change career paths from commercial kitchens to something with more growth opportunity, after coming into contact with TheOdinProject website. Outside of attending classes I also enjoy reading, gaming and cooking for my family members and friends. On this site you will find examples of my projects for you to peruse. Some examples include a recipe page done to practice my skills for class, as well as a recipe book site that is currently a WIP.
